# Changelog

Changes an author would notice, by the date they went live.

## 2026-10-24
- A third model can be chosen for drafting and editing.

## 2026-10-18
- Agent runs continue on the server when you close the page or lose the connection.
- While Weaver is working you can queue a message for the next step or send it right away.
- Auto mode runs within a spending budget and a limit on the number of steps.
- Removed: starting a book from a one-sentence idea. Books start from your outline or an imported manuscript.

## 2026-08-03
- Whole-book check: reads the entire book and reports continuity problems across chapters.

## 2026-08-02
- Loomnovel can be used from Claude Code and other MCP clients. Create a personal token in the settings page and paste one line into your client.

## 2026-07-27
- Editorial review gives comments with reasons and never rewrites your text by itself.

## 2026-07-25
- Usable on phones.

## 2026-07-24
- Terms of service and privacy policy published.

## 2026-07-13
- Import a long manuscript: chapters are split automatically and a worldbook is drafted from the text.

## 2026-07-07
- First production release, invite-only.
