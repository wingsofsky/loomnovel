# Reporting a vulnerability

Please email founder@loomnovel.com with "Security" in the subject. Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it.

- You will get a reply within 5 working days.
- Please do not access, change or delete data that is not yours, and stop as soon as you can show the problem.
- Please do not run load tests or denial-of-service tests against loomnovel.com.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you publish anything.

If you wish, we will credit you in the changelog.
